How the goals are judged

Evidence grammar

Three labels. No ambiguity.

Observed

Broker & paper record

Marks, positions, orders, and drawdown already recorded by the current paper system.

Simulated

Research evidence

Walk-forward, stress, and capacity results under declared data and cost assumptions.

Planned

14-strategy goal

A governed breadth objective. It is not represented as a current portfolio achievement.

One decision / end to end

Follow a rebalance through the entire system.

Each layer answers a different question: what the model knew, what it chose, what the broker accepted, and what was published.

  1. 01 / InputWhat the model could know

    Point-in-time data and survivorship contracts.

    Data contracts
  2. 02 / DecisionWhat was fixed first

    Reserved identity, parameters, and admission rule.

    Trial ledger
  3. 03 / RiskWhat the portfolio allowed

    Weight, exposure, drawdown, and kill state.

    Risk evidence
  4. 04 / BrokerWhat Alpaca accepted

    Positions, orders, account mark, and reconciliation.

    Broker evidence
  5. 05 / RecordWhat cannot be silently replaced

    Signed, append-only public payloads.
