Performance and risk ratios

23

  • return_statsReturn statistics

    Summarize a return series: periods, annualized geometric return (CAGR) and volatility, Sharpe, max drawdown, skew, excess kurtosis, best and worst period, share positive.

    Arguments: returns?: number[], prices?: number[], periods_per_year?: number, risk_free?: number

  • sharpe_ratioSharpe ratio with standard error

    Compute the annualized Sharpe ratio with its standard error and 95% interval, under iid normal returns (Lo 2002) and allowing skew and fat tails (Mertens 2002). For the probability it beats luck across many trials, use canli-validation-mcp.

    Arguments: returns?: number[], prices?: number[], periods_per_year?: number, risk_free?: number

  • sortino_ratioSortino ratio

    Compute the annualized Sortino ratio: mean return above a target over downside deviation below it, with the downside deviation itself.

    Arguments: returns?: number[], prices?: number[], periods_per_year?: number, target?: number

  • max_drawdownDrawdowns

    Find the maximum drawdown and the worst drawdown episodes: depth, peak, trough, recovery and length in periods, plus time under water.

    Arguments: returns?: number[], prices?: number[], periods_per_year?: number, top?: integer, dates?: string[]

  • calmar_ratioCalmar ratio

    Compute the Calmar ratio: annualized geometric return over the absolute maximum drawdown of the whole series.

    Arguments: returns?: number[], prices?: number[], periods_per_year?: number

  • omega_ratioOmega ratio

    Compute the Omega ratio at a threshold: the sum of gains above it over the sum of losses below it.

    Arguments: returns?: number[], prices?: number[], periods_per_year?: number, threshold?: number

  • value_at_riskValue at risk

    Estimate value at risk as a positive loss fraction: historical, Gaussian and Cornish-Fisher (skew and kurtosis adjusted), over one or more periods.

    Arguments: returns?: number[], prices?: number[], periods_per_year?: number, confidence?: number, horizon?: integer

  • expected_shortfallExpected shortfall (CVaR)

    Estimate expected shortfall (CVaR): the average loss beyond value at risk, historical and Gaussian, as a positive loss fraction.

    Arguments: returns?: number[], prices?: number[], periods_per_year?: number, confidence?: number, horizon?: integer

  • capm_regressionAlpha and beta (CAPM)

    Regress excess returns on benchmark excess returns: beta, alpha (annualized), their t-stats and p-values, R-squared, correlation and residual (idiosyncratic) volatility.

  • information_ratioInformation ratio and tracking error

    Arguments: returns?: number[], prices?: number[], periods_per_year?: number, benchmark: number[]

  • treynor_ratioTreynor ratio

    Compute Modigliani's M-squared: the return the strategy would have earned levered to the benchmark's volatility, and its difference from the benchmark.

  • capture_ratiosUp and down capture

    Arguments: returns?: number[], prices?: number[], periods_per_year?: number, benchmark: number[]

  • ulcer_indexUlcer index and Martin ratio

    Compute the Ulcer index (root-mean-square drawdown), the Martin (ulcer performance) ratio, and the pain index and pain ratio (mean drawdown).

    Arguments: returns?: number[], prices?: number[], periods_per_year?: number, risk_free?: number

  • drawdown_at_riskConditional drawdown at risk

    Estimate drawdown at risk (a drawdown quantile across periods) and conditional drawdown at risk (the mean drawdown beyond it), as positive fractions.

    Arguments: returns?: number[], prices?: number[], periods_per_year?: number, confidence?: number

  • tail_ratioTail and Rachev ratios

    Compute the tail ratio (95th percentile return over the absolute 5th) and the Rachev ratio (mean of the best tail over the mean loss of the worst tail).

    Arguments: returns?: number[], prices?: number[], periods_per_year?: number, tail?: number

  • gain_to_painGain-to-pain ratio

  • kelly_fractionKelly fraction

  • autocorrelationAutocorrelation and Ljung-Box

    Arguments: strikes: number[], call_prices: number[], years: number, rate?: number, levels?: number[]

Econometrics and tests

27

  • mean_reversion_fitMean reversion (Ornstein-Uhlenbeck) fit

    Arguments: series: number[], dt?: number

  • garch_volatilityGARCH(1,1) volatility model

  • rolling_betaRolling beta

  • compare_sharpe_ratiosCompare two Sharpe ratios

  • welch_t_testWelch two-sample t-test

    Arguments: sample_a: number[], sample_b: number[]

  • markov_regime_switchingMarkov regime-switching model

    Fit a Hamilton Markov-switching model with a mean and volatility per regime (2 or 3 regimes) by maximum likelihood: regime means and volatilities, transition probabilities, expected durations, the current regime probabilities and how much time was spent in each.

  • johansen_cointegrationJohansen cointegration test

    Test how many cointegrating relationships tie several price series together (Johansen trace and maximum-eigenvalue tests with Osterwald-Lenum critical values), with the rank chosen at 5% and the normalized cointegrating vectors.

  • chow_break_testStructural break (Chow) test

  • har_rv_forecastHAR realized-volatility forecast

  • realized_volatility_measuresRealized volatility and jumps

    From intraday returns per day, compute realized variance, bipower variation, the jump component and the Barndorff-Nielsen-Shephard jump test (tripower quarticity), realized semivariances, realized skewness and kurtosis, and the daily series for HAR forecasts.

    Arguments: intraday_returns: number[][], jump_level?: number, periods_per_year?: number, include_series?: boolean

  • dependence_measuresDependence beyond correlation

    Measure how two series move together: Pearson, Spearman and Kendall correlations with p-values, distance correlation (catches nonlinear dependence), and empirical lower and upper tail dependence (do they crash together?).

    Arguments: x: number[], y: number[], tail_quantile?: number

  • bootstrap_confidence_intervalBlock-bootstrap confidence interval

    Arguments: returns: number[], statistic?: sharpe|sortino|cagr|volatility|..., confidence?: number, reps?: integer, block?: integer, seed?: integer, periods_per_year?: number

    Arguments: prices_file: string, groups_file?: string, dollar_volume_file?: string, signals?: string[], min_names?: integer, smoothing?: integer, cost_bps?: number, target_vol?: number, split_date?: string, out_file?: string, ledger?: string
