canli-markets-mcp: markets, filings and the economy from the sources

canli-markets-mcp is a free, open-source MCP server that reads public market data from the sources themselves: SEC filings, the US Treasury, FRED, the World Bank, central banks, European annual reports, prediction markets, DeFi data and prices. It needs no API key or account, and every result names the URL it came from.

Common questions

Is canli-markets-mcp free?

Yes. It is MIT-licensed and needs no API key and no account. SEC's fair-access policy asks you to set SEC_USER_AGENT to your name and email. Keyless prices come from Yahoo Finance's public chart data, which is unofficial and for personal use; you can use your own Alpaca or Tiingo keys instead.

Where does the data come from?

Each request goes from your machine straight to the source, such as SEC EDGAR, the US Treasury, FRED or the ECB, and each result names the URL it came from, so an agent can cite it.

Which AI assistants can use it?

Claude, Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Windsurf, Cline, Zed and any other MCP client.

Should I install this or canli-mcp?

canli-mcp includes this server and six others, and lists only a few tools up front, so it costs your model far less context. Install this one if you want these tools listed directly and nothing else.

Is it investment advice?

No. It reports what public sources say, and it does not tell anyone what to buy or sell.

canli-markets-mcp 0.17.0 tools
ToolWhat it does
enforcement_actionsThe SEC's latest enforcement: litigation releases and administrative proceedings, with who was named, the number and the document, across the market or filtered to a company or person (every word of the name must match). The feeds hold the latest actions only, so an empty answer is limited to what they reach.
consumer_complaintsComplaints consumers sent the CFPB about a bank, lender, card issuer or credit bureau: totals, the last 12 months, top products and issues, and how often the company answered in time. Complaints are unverified and not adjusted for size.
product_recallsRecalls: a firm's FDA recalls of drugs, devices and food with class, reason and status, and a vehicle's NHTSA safety recalls by make, model and year.
sanctions_checkSearch the U.S. Treasury's Specially Designated Nationals list and its aliases for a person, company, bank or vessel, by whole words. Not a compliance screening: no match is not clearance, and a match needs checking against the entry.
federal_rulesFederal Register documents: final and proposed rules, notices and presidential documents by words and agency (SEC, CFTC, Fed, Treasury, CFPB, FDA and more), with comment deadlines, effective dates and abstracts. Can list only rules still open for comment.
technical_indicatorsThe common technical indicators for a symbol in one call, from its daily prices: moving averages (SMA and EMA), RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands and ATR, with where the close sits against its 50- and 200-day averages. Standard definitions; describes past prices, not a forecast.
stock_snapshotWhat a quote page shows and no primary source publishes, from Yahoo Finance: analysts' mean, median, high and low price targets, how many analysts, the recommendation and ratings, EPS and revenue estimates by quarter and year, market cap, trailing and forward P/E and other ratios, 52-week range, dividend rate, short interest, institutional and insider percentages, next earnings date. Vendor aggregates, labeled as such; for reported figures use the filings.
security_identifiersMap a ticker, ISIN, CUSIP, SEDOL or FIGI to one security through OpenFIGI: its name, FIGIs, listings, exchange and type. Check digits are tested first, so a mistyped ISIN or CUSIP is refused.
legal_entityWho a company legally is, from the Global LEI register: legal name, LEI, jurisdiction, status, addresses, former names, its direct and ultimate parents and how many entities it owns. By name or LEI; works for firms worldwide, listed or not.
world_yieldsGovernment bond yields side by side: U.S. Treasury, euro area (ECB, AAA), UK (Bank of England), Japan (Ministry of Finance) and Canada (Bank of Canada), by maturity, with the date and basis of each curve, 10Y-2Y slopes and 10-year spreads to the U.S.
credit_cycleThe BIS credit cycle by country: private credit as a share of GDP, its long-run trend and the credit-to-GDP gap, an early-warning measure regulators use for banking stress. Up to 40 quarters.
dividend_historyA security's dividends and splits: each payment by ex-date, totals by year, the trailing 12-month yield, five-year growth and the streak of rising annual totals. Works for foreign listings.
verify_claimsCheck a draft answer's figures against the company's own filings before using them: revenue, net income, EPS, operating income, margins, growth, cash flow, assets, debt and more, for a fiscal year or quarter as the company labels it. US and foreign filers (20-F, IFRS), in their own currency. Each claim comes back confirmed, contradicted (with the filed figure and what the claimed one matches instead: another year, a quarter, a unit slip), not reported or not checkable.
exposure_mapWho depends on a company, from what others say in their annual reports: every filer that names it, with the passage, and whether it reads as a supplier, customer or competitor. For supply chains no fund report shows. Read by rules, so check the passage.
guidance_recordA management team's record against its own word: each quarter it gave guidance in its press release, what it said and what then happened, how often results came in above, within and below its own range, and which quarters fell short.
what_happened_nextEvery past time a market or stock fell, rose or sat this far below its high: each case, what followed a month, a quarter and a year later, the worst and best, and how that compares with an ordinary day. A record, not a forecast or advice.
filing_memoryEverything one company has said about a subject across every annual report it has on EDGAR, back to the 1990s where they exist: the best passages with links, how often it came up each year, and optionally how that moved with a margin, tested against chance. Reads the reports on this machine; no key. Supply the company's likely words in also and all_of, since a company rarely uses yours.
filing_sentimentThe tone of a filing or any text, sentence by sentence, from FinBERT run on this machine: an 8-K's press release by default, or a 10-K section. Needs the optional local models (canli-mcp models install). Tone, not truth, and no evidence it predicts a price; the model is licensed for non-commercial use.
shipping_activityShips moving through the world's straits and canals (Suez, Panama, Malacca, Hormuz and others) and ports, from the IMF's PortWatch, which estimates them from satellite tracking of ships. All chokepoints against a year ago, one chokepoint by week, or one port. No key.
page_attentionDaily Wikipedia page views for a company or subject: a plain measure of public attention, with how the last week compares with normal. No key.
latest_priceThe latest price of a stock, ETF, index, currency or crypto pair, with the previous close and the time of the price. No key; can run late, by 15 minutes or more on some markets.
european_filingsA European company's annual financial report as it filed it in the European Single Electronic Format (ESEF): revenue, operating and net profit, EPS, assets, equity, cash, cash flows and dividends as the company tagged them (IFRS concepts, its currency, full scale), with the comparatives, the report's viewer link and the other years on file. Issuers on EU and EEA regulated markets, found by name or LEI; words find any other tagged figure.
reference_ratesMoney-market and policy rates and the ECB's euro exchange reference rates (fx:GBP) from the central banks themselves: SOFR, TGCR, BGCR, EFFR and OBFR from the New York Fed (with percentiles and volume), the euro short-term rate and the ECB's policy rates, SONIA and Bank Rate from the Bank of England, and about forty central banks' policy rates as the BIS collects them (policy:JP, policy:Brazil). Dates and rates, oldest first.
prediction_marketsWhat traders on Polymarket and Kalshi are paying for an outcome, found by words (a Fed decision, a recession, an election, a price level): each market's probability (the middle of bid and ask, else the last trade), bid, ask, volume and closing date, with the event's resolution rules.
crypto_fundamentalsA DeFi protocol's total value locked (now, by chain, and its history), the fees users pay it and the revenue it keeps (24 hours to all time); a chain's total value locked; or a stablecoin's supply in circulation and its change, by chain. From DefiLlama's open data.
financial_statementsA company's financial statements exactly as filed: income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and equity statement from its 10-K (or 10-Q), with its own line labels, each line's XBRL concept and the values as the filing shows them (units say the scale). Joins several filings by period; a line a later filing restated, or that a stock split changed, is shown beside its first-reported figure, never mixed in.
filing_tablesThe table in a filing that holds a figure, found by words ("paid members", "revenue by region", "guidance"): XBRL statement and detail reports and the tables in the document itself (MD&A figures such as members, stores or ARPU), or an 8-K press release's tables. With filings above one, the same words in each earlier filing, to follow a figure over the years. Without words, lists the tables.
earnings_releaseA quarter's results press release (8-K Item 2.02): the headline, the results it states (each with the part of the business it belongs to and what a change is measured against), its guidance parsed into ranges (low, high, midpoint, unit, measure, basis, period, and the sentence or table row), and the previous release's guidance for this quarter set against the company's results: above, within or below the range, in basis points for rates.
release_checkA quarter's results press release set against the 10-Q or 10-K filed after it: every figure in the release's tables looked for among the report's inline XBRL facts for the same period. Lists the figures that changed (release value, filed value, difference, percent, the XBRL concept and how the line was matched), release figures with no counterpart (non-GAAP measures, metrics) and lines the report adds, such as a charge booked after the release.
audit_signalsWhat auditors and companies say when something is wrong, from SEC filings. Going-concern doubt: each 10-K and 10-Q whose text says there is substantial doubt about the company's ability to continue, as a timeline (first raised, still raised, lifted), or across the market the companies whose latest report newly raised it. Auditor changes (8-K Item 4.01): who left and who came, resigned or dismissed, disagreements and reportable events. The latest 10-K's critical audit matters. Each row quotes the filing.
screen_companiesScreen every US-listed company (thousands, from SEC XBRL frames) on fundamentals: revenue, growth, margins, ROE, ROA, free cash flow, R&D intensity, leverage and more, for a calendar year or each company's fiscal year ending in a date window, with filters, ranking and optional valuation (price, market cap, P/E, P/S, FCF yield). Implausible figures (tagging slips) are held out and listed apart.
company_reportOne company in one call: profile, five fiscal years of financials (revenue, growth, margins, net income, EPS, cash flow, ROE, leverage), price and risk over a year (return, volatility, drawdown, beta), valuation (market cap, P/E, P/S, FCF yield), insider buying and selling, and recent filings, each from its source.
event_studyHow a stock's price reacted to events: earnings releases (8-K item 2.02), insider purchases or sales (Form 4), any 8-K, or given dates. Abnormal returns against a market model, cumulative abnormal return per event and on average, with a t test across events. For filings use event_type (with last for the most recent few), not filing dates: it starts day 0 at the first session after the SEC accepted the filing.
mentions_trendHow often a word or phrase appears in SEC filings over time (by month, quarter or year), across all filers or one company, from EDGAR full-text search: for themes such as "tariffs", "agentic AI" or "going concern".
company_profileA company's or fund's SEC profile by ticker, CIK or name: tickers, exchange, industry (SIC), fiscal year end, state, address, former names, and its latest 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K and 13F.
list_filingsA company's SEC filings, newest first, filtered by form type (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, DEF 14A, S-1, 4, 13F-HR...) and dates, with links. Use read_filing to read one.
read_filingThe text of an SEC filing: the latest of a form, or one by accession. For a 10-K or 10-Q, section returns one item ("risk factors", "md&a", "business", "market risk", "legal proceedings", or a number such as "1A"); without it the result lists the items and their lengths. document reads an exhibit, such as an 8-K's press release ("EX-99.1"). find returns only the passages with given words ("employees", "buyback"), the fastest way to a fact in a long filing; otherwise long text pages with offset.
search_filingsFull-text search across all SEC filings since 2001 (EDGAR full-text search): which companies mention a phrase, filtered by form, filer and dates. Quote phrases.
insider_tradesInsider transactions at a company from its Form 4 filings: who (officers, directors, 10% owners), what (open-market buys and sales, option exercises, tax withholding, gifts), shares, price, value, holdings after, and 10b5-1 plan flags, with a buy/sell summary. Default: the last 90 days.
fund_holdingsA fund manager's 13F holdings for a quarter (latest by default): positions by value with portfolio weights, the total checked against the filing's cover page, and what changed since the previous quarter (new, exited, added, reduced).
unusual_optionsUnusual options activity for a stock, ETF or index from Cboe's delayed quotes: contracts trading at least as many times their open interest as asked (new positions more likely than closing), ranked by the premium that changed hands, with strike, expiry, IV and delta, and the day's call and put premium.
company_emissionsA company's greenhouse-gas emissions from its US facilities as they reported them to EPA (the Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program): direct emissions in tonnes of CO2e, the company's ownership share of them, by gas, the largest facilities, and a trend over up to five years. Scope 1 from the measurements, not a vendor's ESG score.
crypto_marketsThe crypto market from CoinGecko: coins ranked by market value with price, volume, change over a day, a week and a month, supply and distance from the all-time high, and the whole market's value with bitcoin's and ether's shares; or the coins named, by symbol or CoinGecko id.
etf_profileWhat an ETF or mutual fund costs and holds, from Yahoo Finance's fund profile: the expense ratio beside its category's average, turnover, total assets, yield, year-to-date and five-year returns, beta, inception, top holdings with weights, the mix of stocks, bonds and cash, and sector weights.
market_calendarThe whole market's schedule from Nasdaq's public calendars: who reports earnings on given days (before the open or after the close, consensus EPS and number of estimates, last year's EPS), who goes ex-dividend (amount, record and payment dates), stock splits coming up, and a month's IPOs (priced, expected, filed, withdrawn); filter by tickers or market value.
congress_tradesStock and bond trades by members of the House, read from their periodic transaction reports on the Clerk's site: owner (self, spouse, joint, child), asset and ticker, purchase or sale, trade date, date notified and the amount range, for one member, a state or district, a ticker, or the newest reports filed, with each report's link.
analyst_historyA stock's analyst history from Yahoo Finance: every upgrade, downgrade, initiation and target change by firm over any window, the last four quarters' earnings surprises, how the EPS estimate moved over 90 days with the analysts revising it up and down, and the rating counts month by month. stock_snapshot has today's targets and estimates. A vendor's aggregates, labeled so.
market_moversToday's biggest US stock gainers, losers and most traded (price, change, volume, average volume, market value), or Yahoo Finance's other ready-made lists: small-cap gainers, most shorted, undervalued growth, growth technology, aggressive small caps, undervalued large caps.
market_hoursThe US stock market's calendar (NYSE and Nasdaq): open, pre-market, after-hours or closed now, the next open and close in Eastern time and UTC, a year's holidays and 1 p.m. early closes, whether a date trades, and how many trading days lie between two dates. Computed from NYSE's rules and its unscheduled closures since 2000, with no network.
factor_returnsThe Fama-French factors from Kenneth French's data library at Dartmouth: the market over the T-bill (Mkt-RF), size (SMB), value (HML), profitability (RMW), investment (CMA) and momentum (Mom), with the one-month T-bill rate (RF), monthly since 1926, daily or by year, as fractions ready for a factor regression.
scenario_riskA portfolio's holdings under a macro shock or a past crisis. Shock some of eight factors (US stocks, the 2- and 10-year Treasury yields, the Baa-Aaa spread, oil, the dollar, gold, the VIX) and the rest move as history says they do alongside, with a 90% range; or replay a crisis (2008, 2020, 2022 and eight more) beside each holding's actual return then, with the model's error. Betas from weekly returns over the last years. Local server (prices).
trade_ticksEvery trade on Kraken's public market for a crypto pair (BTC/USD, ETH/EUR, SOL/USD) from any moment back to the market's opening, tick by tick: time to the microsecond, price, size, which side took liquidity and the order type, with the VWAP, buying against selling, the largest trades and one-minute bars built from the ticks. Up to 10,000 trades a call; next_since reads on exactly where it stopped. No key.
trade_flowsWhat countries buy from and sell to each other, by product, from UN Comtrade: who supplies a country's chips, oil or soybeans and with what share, or how one partner's share moved year by year (in US dollars and kilograms). Products by HS code or plain words. Names Taiwan (listed as Other Asia, nes) and says how much of a total names no partner, which customs offices sometimes withhold. Goods, customs figures a year or two behind. No key.
standardized_financialsA company's income statement, balance sheet and cash flow in one template whether it files US GAAP or IFRS, 26 lines with 14 ratios, each figure saying which tag it came from or the arithmetic that made it. One company over up to 10 fiscal years, or up to five side by side (Apple beside TSMC beside SAP), optionally in US dollars at each year's average and year-end rates. From annual reports at SEC, restatements applied.
bond_offeringsThe bonds a company sold, tranche by tranche, from the SEC filings that priced them: amount, coupon, maturity, price, yield, spread over Treasuries (mid-swaps for euro bonds, the index for floating notes), benchmark and CUSIP from each final term sheet, with the XBRL fee tables of final prospectuses confirming amounts and adding deals priced without one. Each dollar yield is recomputed from its price. Yearly totals by currency. Registered deals only.
financial_cycleWhere countries stand in their financial cycle, from the Bank for International Settlements: house prices (real and nominal, with the change on a year), the share of income households and companies spend servicing debt, credit to households, companies and government as a share of GDP, and real and nominal effective exchange rates. Up to eight countries side by side, 40 to 60 economies covered, with history. No key.
treasury_cashThe Treasury's cash at the Fed each business day since 2005 (the Treasury General Account, from the Daily Treasury Statement), its change and range over the window, and optionally the last day's largest deposits and withdrawals by category (taxes withheld, debt issued and redeemed, interest, Social Security) with month and fiscal year to date.
fed_liquidityThe Fed's footprint in the money markets: the overnight reverse repo facility's daily take-up and the standing repo facility's lending (New York Fed, its test trades kept apart), the Fed's securities holdings week by week with the pace they change by type, and its balance sheet: total assets, reserve balances, the TGA and net liquidity (assets less the TGA and the reverse repo). Each with changes over a week, four weeks and a year, a plain summary first; or any of them day by day or week by week. No key.
money_marketsThe US money markets' plumbing, no key: overnight repo by segment (SOFR, TGCR, BGCR, tri-party without the Fed, GCF, cleared bilateral DVP), each rate with its volume and its spread to the Fed's interest on reserves and overnight reverse repo rate; what US money market funds hold by type and whom their repo is with (Form N-MFP, via the OFR); and primary dealers' net Treasury positions by maturity (New York Fed). part gives one with its history.
inflation_nowcastThe Cleveland Fed's daily nowcast of inflation not yet published: its estimate of CPI, core CPI, PCE and core PCE for the coming months and the quarter, on the month, on a year and annualized, beside the last month published. month gives one month's nowcast day by day and its figure once out; record sets the last nowcast before each release against the published figure, with the average miss. A plain summary first. No key.
rate_expectationsWhat the futures market expects the Fed to do: from 30-day fed funds futures, the rate implied after each scheduled FOMC meeting, the expected change there and from today, the chances of no change, a hike or a cut, and of each target range after each meeting, by the method CME's FedWatch publishes, with the contracts behind it and today's range and effective rate. A plain summary first. No key.

A plain summary first. No key.
housing_marketThe US housing market, nationally or for a metro area: Zillow's typical home value with its change on a month, a year and five years and its distance from the peak; Realtor.com's listings (median listing price, active and new listings, days on market, the share with a price cut, pending sales), each against a year earlier; and the 30-year mortgage rate. rank lists the largest metros by how fast home values rise or fall. consumer_expectationsWhat American households expect, from the New York Fed's Survey of Consumer Expectations, monthly: inflation over one, three and five years, home prices, the prices of gas, food, rent, medical care and college, earnings, income and spending, and the chances of losing or finding a job, of higher unemployment, of missing a debt payment and of stocks rising; the latest month beside a month and a year before, or one topic month by month. No key.
margin_debtHow much investors borrow against their stocks, from FINRA's monthly margin statistics since 1997: margin debt (customers' debit balances at member firms) with its change on a month and a year, the record and how far the latest is from it, the free credit balances in cash and margin accounts, and the months before. A plain summary first. No key.
options_sentimentHow much protection options traders buy, from Cboe's daily market statistics: the total, equity, index, ETF, VIX and SPX put/call ratios of the latest trading day with the calls and puts traded, and the total, equity, index and SPX ratios day by day with their 10- and 20-day averages. A plain summary first. No key.
recession_watchThe indicators read for a US recession, each beside the line it is read against and whether it is past it: the New York Fed's yield-curve model (the chance of being in recession twelve months ahead, computed as the New York Fed publishes it, month by month), the Sahm rule, the 10-year less 2-year curve and its last inversion, jobless claims against a year earlier, the Chauvet-Piger probability, GDPNow, and the Fed Board staff's excess bond premium model. A plain summary first. No key.
state_economyA US state's economy beside the nation's: the unemployment rate, payroll jobs, the Philadelphia Fed's coincident index of activity, house prices (FHFA) and real GDP, each with its change on a year; or every state and DC ranked by unemployment or by the growth of jobs, activity or house prices. A plain summary first. No key.
inflation_breakdownWhat is driving US consumer prices, from the BLS's CPI: all items and core beside food (at home and away), energy and gasoline, core goods and used cars, core services, shelter, rent, owners' equivalent rent, medical care and transportation services, each on the month (seasonally adjusted), over three months at an annual rate and on a year as the BLS states it, with the headline and core month by month. A plain summary first. No key.
fed_speechesWhat the Federal Reserve Board says, from its own feeds: the latest speeches and testimony by its members and its monetary policy releases (statements, minutes), with speaker, title, date, venue and link; one member's; or one speech read, its paragraphs on monetary policy and the economy quoted as given. A plain summary first. No key.
central_bank_speechesWhat central bankers everywhere are saying, from the BIS's collection of their speeches: the latest fifty with speaker, role and bank, title, date and link; one bank's (ECB, Bank of England, Bundesbank, Bank of Japan...) or one speaker's; or one speech read, brief excerpts of its passages on policy. A plain summary first. No key.
financial_conditionsHow loose or tight US financial conditions are and how much stress markets show, in one answer: the OFR's daily Financial Stress Index with what credit, equity valuation, safe assets, funding and volatility each add; the Chicago Fed's NFCI and its parts; the St. Louis and Kansas City Feds' stress indexes; corporate bond spreads by rating (ICE BofA), Moody's Baa spread and the Fed Board's excess bond premium; the VIX. Each with its changes and its place in its own history.
market_breadthHow many of an index's members take part in its moves, computed from the members themselves: the share of the S&P 500's (or the S&P 400's, the Dow's, or one sector's) members above their 50- and 200-day averages, how many rose and fell, new 52-week highs and lows, how far the typical member is below its high, the average member's return beside the index's own over a day, a month, the year to date and a year, recent sessions' breadth, and by_sector for the eleven sectors.
labor_marketThe US jobs picture in one answer: payroll gains with the three-month average, the unemployment rate and U-6, participation and the prime-age employment ratio, wages and hours, JOLTS openings, quits and layoffs with openings per unemployed person, and weekly jobless claims, each beside the reading before and a year before, with payroll gains month by month. A plain summary first. No key.
commodity_pricesWorld commodity prices from the World Bank's Pink Sheet, monthly since 1960: oil, gas, LNG and coal, metals and precious metals, grains, oilseeds, coffee, cocoa, sugar, cotton, rubber, timber and fertilizers, each with its change on a month, a year and five years, beside the World Bank's sector indexes; or one commodity month by month with its record; all for every commodity.
