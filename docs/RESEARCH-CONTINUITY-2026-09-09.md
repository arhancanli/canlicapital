# Lower-page continuity pass

The full United Carriers reference journey was compared with the local page,
including its crane/container, road, ship, aircraft and editorial closing stages.
The important missing relationship was continuity between lower sections, not
the absence of individual entrance animations. Reference evidence and this
pass's art direction are in artifacts/reference/united-carriers/DESIGN.md.

## Implemented

- One original CSS 3D glass research packet persists alongside research,
  evidence, accountability, questions and access. Layers separate and reunite
  on scroll; stage color and orientation change without hiding product content.
- A dedicated wide-screen composition reserves space for the artwork. Evidence
  grids reflow to two columns. On mobile, short windows and reduced-motion
  preferences, the illustration is removed and the full-width reading layout
  remains. The illustration is explicitly conceptual, excluded from assistive
  technology and has no relationship to live trading status.
- Research links become editorial rows, replacing repeated optical-image cards.
- The final access scene receives at least one viewport of room so its heading
  and illustration can be seen together before the existing footer handoff.
