# Lower chapters — design plan

Scope: the user's named sections from Evidence Core through FAQ. Preserve source
text, counts, claim boundaries, links and original product controls. Do not
reinterpret dated data as current or make objectives look achieved.

Tokens: carbon #000000; paper #f4f4f4; ink #111111; cobalt #0016cb;
copper #ff5500; silver #bac3ce. Chakra Petch display, Inter reading, IBM Plex
Mono provenance. Large readable headings; quieter timestamps and labels.

Composition:

    Evidence core   [large title / explanation]
                    [full-width actual trial field]
                    [five selectable states + return to scroll]
    System films    [film selector]
                    [one full-width real film + complete artifact notes]
    Decision trace  [large heading]
                    [carried marker → five full-width provenance stations]
    Research        [moving archive landscape]
                    [three distinct paper covers, actual titles and links]
    Evidence        [large observed count | four operating-fact rows]
                    [separate objective / model-estimate register]
                    [signed receipt / readable broker ledger]
    Grammar         [three clearly differentiated evidence labels]
    Authorship      [black colophon / oversized named builder / three contracts]
    FAQ             [cobalt question room / large full-width disclosures]

Critique: another narrow animation sidebar or the same small card repeated eight
times misses the brief. Instead the diagram, cinema, route, library, ledger,
colophon and question room each have a different reading structure. Signature:
the information itself becomes the scene, not an unrelated 3D decoration.

Motion: explicit state controls make the core explorable; preserve its actual
trial-data renderer. Film selection respects native buttons and keyboard tabs.
The route carrier follows the document's five steps; paper covers settle with
scroll. FAQ answers enter gently. No autoplay audio, new fictitious metric or
inaccessible scroll trapping. Reduced motion and no-JS retain readable content.
