- Leads: It is the only keyless server that gives the SEC value as it stood on a date, flags a later restatement, and returns the accession number and a hash-checked source. Example, Apple FY2018 diluted EPS as of 2019-01-01: ours returns 11.91 (filed 2018-11-05, changed_after=true). cyanheads shows the split-restated 2.98 next to the pre-split FY2017 9.21 with no flag, and pit-fundamentals' 'revenue' returns a $45.4B quarter where ours gives FY2018 $265.6B. The data matches the SEC exactly: 102,134 periods over 8 companies with 0 differences, and 6,503 history rows and 6,020 property checks with 0 failures. Of 716 finance registry entries, only this one mentions restatements.
- Trails: Four rules give wrong answers without warning. (1) history ignores as_of unless basis is also set, so AAPL as of 2010 returns a 2025 row, which is a lookahead leak. (2) 16- and 17-week quarters are classed 'other', so Kroger quarterly as of 2026-07-15 returns -1.32B instead of +903M. (3) Companies that switched to IFRS go stale silently: for Toyota as of 2025-12-31 all 10 defaults end 2020-03-31, and Sony's end 2021-03-31. (4) restatements lumps split re-denomination (-75% EPS), 424B2 fee facts and A-B-A changes together. It needs exact XBRL tags. history 'revenue' errors, and 'Revenues' gives Apple 3 years with FY2017 first_reported a year late, while cyanheads, edgartools and pit accept friendly names. There is no cross-company tool (cyanheads frames, edgartools compare and valuein PIT tables exist). There is no name or former-ticker lookup: TWTR is refused, and XOM is missing from the 6,415-ticker reference. There are no derived ratios or TTM. Data comes from a snapshot about 8 days old, not live. The first call per company takes 404-707 ms, or 1.5-2.2 s on a CDN miss, against pipeworx's 75-224 ms. Heap grows from 16 to 129 MB over 9 filers and RSS peaks at 270 MB, against 137 MB for pipeworx and 192 MB for secedgar. list_concepts and restatements can return 46-51k chars. It is not hosted, has no provenance, has no repo of its own, is not on Glama and is not in npm's top 250 for 'sec edgar mcp'.
