# Design review of the company reference pages, September 23, 2026

Status: review complete; the template polish is in the pull request that carries
this file. Screenshots, per-element measurements and the capture scripts are kept
outside the repository (session scratchpad, `design/`), so this record lists the
findings and what changed, not the images.

## Method

Eight live pages (home, directory, company overview, a history with a
stated-condition notice, a plain history, filing index, filing page,
historical-filer overview) captured with Playwright at 1440×900 and 390×844 with a
desktop Chrome user agent; full-page and fold screenshots plus 147 viewport tiles;
computed styles collected for every element (font families, colours, alignment,
widths); the shared motion script probed at 0.5 s, 3.5 s and after a scroll. The
reviewer ranked 21 defects. A second capture of the polished template ran against
the v27 preview server on this branch at both widths.

## Findings and disposition

High
1. Company pages ran on a different type and colour system from the rest of the
   site (Instrument Sans on a blue-tinted grid, seven font-family strings, eleven
   text greys and blues, a third link blue). Fixed: the template now uses the
   reading-surface tokens shared with the research and hub pages (neutral paper,
   Inter, Chakra Petch 500 for headings, one link blue, IBM Plex Mono for labels
   and code); the grid is gone.
2. Six-column tables clipped the value, unit and filed columns at phone width
   with no affordance. Fixed: sticky first column, an edge fade that appears only
   when the table overflows, tighter cells at 650 px and below.
3. The scroll-drawn section rule stalled part-way for chapters already in view at
   load and sat on top of the section's own border, leaving a two-tone line.
   Fixed: chapters in view at load draw their rule at once (shared motion
   script); the template hides its static border where the drawn rule exists.
4. The decorative grid showed through every table cell. Fixed with 1 (grid
   removed) and an opaque white table surface.
5. A dead band above the content (breadcrumb 163 px under the header at desktop,
   125 px at phone). Fixed: 56 px / 32 px; the h1 now sits at 173 px from the top
   of the viewport at desktop and 141 px at phone.

Medium
6. Directory rows: the CIK column wandered between two- and three-child rows.
   Fixed: one grid for every row.
7. Numeric columns were left-aligned. Fixed: value, count and day cells and their
   headers carry a numeric class and align right.
8. "At date" printed as a period-start value on instant rows. Fixed: a muted
   hyphen (the site's writing contract keeps em dashes out of product pages).
9. H1 formula and title/h1 disagreement. Not changed: titles are indexed and the
   heading text is the page's keyword carrier; the h1 is smaller, balanced, and
   dates and form names inside it no longer break at their hyphens.
10. Paragraph measure of about 98 characters at desktop. Fixed: 70ch.
11. Code and pre fell back to the browser monospace. Fixed: Plex Mono.
12. Raw ISO capture instant in the provenance list and dates breaking at hyphens.
    Fixed for the capture instant (a time element, "2026-09-19 14:42 UTC",
    unbroken); dates inside prose are unchanged.
13. The related-histories list repeated the company name on every row. Fixed:
    the company is named once in the heading; the list flows in columns.
14. Filing pages: one auto-sized table per measure with drifting columns. Fixed:
    fixed column widths shared by every measure table; the per-measure structure
    is kept because the meaning paragraphs are the page's explanatory content.
15. No footer link to the company reference. Not in this change: the footer is
    the shared product shell and is synced into every static page; it takes its
    own pull request.
16. Directory pagination read as prose. Fixed: pager links styled as a control row.

Low
17. Breadcrumb underlined, lowercase leaf. Fixed: no underline, capitalised leaf.
18. Underlined link column in tables. Fixed: underline on hover and focus.
19. Historical-filer notice carried section weight. Fixed: a compact note.
20. Default disclosure marker. Fixed: a plus/minus marker in the site's mono.
21. Sub-10 px text in the shared handoff strip. Not in this change (shared chrome).

Contrast passed AA on every sampled text before and after; no page produces a
horizontal scrollbar at 390 px before or after.

## Verification on this branch

The company test suite passed (204 tests) after the static pilot pages were
regenerated by scripts/build-company-reference.mjs and the filing-page fact
assertion was updated to the numeric cell markup. The polished template was served
by the local preview server on the v27 inputs and measured at both widths: body
Inter, h1 Chakra Petch 500, no background image, numbers right-aligned, code in
Plex Mono, rules full width for chapters in the first fold, sticky first column
and edge fade active on overflowing tables at 390 px, document scroll width equal
to the viewport on every page.
