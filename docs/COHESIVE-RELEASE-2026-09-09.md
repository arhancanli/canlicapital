# Cohesive release pass

Subject: Canli's own systematic strategies, public paper evidence and developer
validation tools. Audience: researchers and developers evaluating the work. Job:
let them inspect the strategy, its record and the tools without losing the thread.

Design: carbon #000000, paper #f4f4f4, ink #111111, cobalt #0016cb,
copper #ff5500, silver #bac3ce. Chakra Petch display, Inter reading,
IBM Plex Mono for evidence and utility. The same optical sculpture from the
opening persists beside the entire process-to-access journey. The actual evidence
visualization and films remain product demonstrations, not decorative substitutes.

Rejected: another isolated footer animation; a new metaphor; copying freight
assets; full-screen scroll takeovers over the evidence. Keep wide-screen
continuity in a dedicated column and full-width mobile/static reading.

## Release boundary

This checkout has no .vercel project link. Do not publish the entire dirty
worktree or overwrite current data with generated pages. Integration needs a
reviewed frontend/generator diff against the publishing checkout, regeneration
from its current artifacts, and staged key issuance, receipt persistence,
rate-limit, release-note delivery, deployed headers, redirects and rollback
checks. Figma and physical-device review have not been updated in this pass.
Read-only inspection confirms the original checkout is on
design/claude-design-20260906 with existing edits including index.html and
founder.html. Those edits were not overwritten; integration must reconcile them.

Final motion verdict: the identified mismatched concepts, viewport repaint and
keyboard animation issues are resolved. Local checks approve this implementation
for visual review; they do not establish exact reference parity or live readiness.
