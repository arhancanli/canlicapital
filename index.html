R/02

Research protocol

An idea earns its place once.

Every candidate enters through the same chain of custody. Variants remain counted, failed curves remain stored, and a new sleeve only changes the portfolio prospectively.

  1. 01

    Pre-register

    Mechanism, variants, costs, holdout and kill rule are fixed before the final data is seen.

  2. 02

    Walk forward

    Point-in-time inputs, purged windows, next-session execution and one shared cost authority.

  3. 03

    Deflate

    Every distinct hypothesis raises the hurdle. DSR and PBO are evaluated across the real search.

  4. 04

    Stress

    Capacity, crisis correlation, beta, turnover and implementation shortfall are part of the result.

  5. 05

    Shadow deploy

    Research survivors transact in isolation before they can join the dated live portfolio.

Q/03

Planned Discovery frontier

The next sleeves must come from somewhere else.

The active queue targets independent economic mechanisms, not more lookbacks on the same signal. The objective is an honest forward Sharpe of 1.5 — stated forward because this book’s own gap between backtest and honest forward runs 1.5× to 3×, and a target quoted in-sample is quoted in the units that flatter. A 2.0–2.5 range was published before and is withdrawn: reaching it would have required an average pairwise correlation at 81% of the mathematical floor.

OPEN BOOK / 2026

Public research record

Trust is an output of the system.

The open book exposes the portfolio logic, sleeve monographs, statistical gauntlet, correction record and raw artifacts needed to reproduce the published state.

The record comes before the pitch, and we mean that literally. Every strategy we killed is published as its own paper, carrying the numbers it died on, beside the handful that survived. So is the arithmetic showing that our own admission gate does not yet reach our own target — because a target published without the sum that bounds it is a pitch, not a record.

That is the whole wager, and it is deliberately not the highest number we could print. A Sharpe of 2.5 is easy to produce and impossible to check: nobody shows you the forty-six ideas that failed on the way, or the window they chose to display, or which of their gates could never have rejected anything. We aim at an honest forward 1.5 and publish the machinery instead — the kill log, the trial budget, the gates with their mutation tests, the corrections we have had to make against ourselves. A number you can verify at 1.5 is rarer than one you cannot at 2.5.
