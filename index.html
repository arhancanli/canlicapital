Canli Capital checks claims about public companies against their SEC filings.

  • What it checks Figures a company reports: revenue, profit, earnings per share, assets, cash and debt.
  • What it can't check Forecasts, opinions, adjusted figures and companies that don't file with the SEC.
  • What it isn't Advice. It shows what was filed, not what to buy.
