R/02

Research protocol

An idea earns its place once.

Every candidate enters through the same chain of custody. Variants remain counted, failed curves remain stored, and a new sleeve only changes the portfolio prospectively.

  1. 01

    Pre-register

    Mechanism, variants, costs, holdout and kill rule are fixed before the final data is seen.

  2. 02

    Walk forward

    Point-in-time inputs, purged windows, next-session execution and one shared cost authority.

  3. 03

    Deflate

    Every distinct hypothesis raises the hurdle. DSR and PBO are evaluated across the real search.

  4. 04

    Stress

    Capacity, crisis correlation, beta, turnover and implementation shortfall are part of the result.

  5. 05

    Shadow deploy

    Research survivors transact in isolation before they can join the dated live portfolio.
