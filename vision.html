The vision

Every financial number, with its source.

Canli Capital builds free, open tools that read the numbers companies publish and check them, for people deciding about their money and for the AI assistants that now help them. It starts with checking. It grows into tools for the companies that make the numbers, and later into funds.

The product lines

From the numbers investors read to the books companies keep

Each starts as a product line under the Canli Capital name and becomes its own company once it has paying customers. The label on each says what exists today.

  • Live

  • Live

    Research that keeps its failures, and a library of strategy sleeves developers can build on.

  • In development

    Canli Books

    An open-source ledger that AI agents and apps can keep books in: every entry balances, a posted entry never changes and every balance traces to its entries. The first release is planned for December 2026.

  • Planned, from December 2026

    A free check of a filing's tagged numbers before it goes in, built on the errors found in real filings; later, the tagged statements themselves.

  • Planned, spring 2027

    Canli Audit

    Analytics for auditors and audit committees: journal-entry tests, Benford's law and restatement risk. Analytics only; audit opinions need licensed auditors.

  • Planned, mid-2027

    Canli Treasury and Planning

    Cash forecasts, budgets and scenarios straight from the books.

  • Paper only

    ALPHAC

    The open-source engine that combines many weakly related strategies. A public paper record, not funded.

  • Later

    The funds

    Funds run from the US, only after licensing, a legal entity and a forward record that justifies them.

How they fit

One engine underneath

Every number keeps its source and every check is recorded, in each product. A figure recorded in Books is closed in Close, filed through Report, tested by Audit, planned in Planning and read by investors in Markets, so each new product starts mostly built.

  1. Now to mid-2027

    Tools developers pay for

    Finance MCP servers measured on independent tests; the first paid developer tier; the sleeve library opening to developers; ALPHAC adding new, uncorrelated families with a year of forward record.

  2. 2027

    The research platform

    The training environment and testing servers, the expert annotation network, and ALPHAC across asset classes with far more independent bets.

  3. 2028

    Groundwork for the funds

    The US company, counsel and compliance, and a real-money track record through the legal path, before any fund opens.

The research side

Five branches, one destination

  • Live

    MCP servers and API

    Finance tools for AI agents: primary-source data and quant tools behind one MCP server, for as few tokens as possible, measured on tests other people wrote.

    canli-mcp and the servers
  • Started

    Data and annotation

    Financial datasets to train and test AI models, computed from public filings and checked by qualified people. The aim is expert annotation that experts in finance trust.

    FilingFacts results
  • Started

    The sleeve library

    A library where developers and their agents build, train and test millions of strategy sleeves, with honest statistics on every result. Failed experiments are kept and used, and developers get their own testing servers later.

    The quant server
  • Paper only

    ALPHAC, the breadth engine

    One book built from hundreds of thousands of sleeves. What matters most is how little they move together: the number of independent bets, not the number of sleeves, sets the ceiling, so a sleeve may be weak as long as it is weakly correlated with the rest and clears its own costs. The target is a Sharpe ratio far beyond 2, claimed only on a forward record after costs.

    The paper record
  • Started

    The breakthroughs lab

    New mathematics and statistics for quant research: tests that tell a real edge from luck, published openly so anyone can check them.

    Research
  • Paper only

    The flagship funds

    The destination all five branches feed: funds run from the US, under US rules, once licensing, a legal entity and a forward record allow it.

Lines we do not cross

What Canli Capital will not do

  • Investment advice, or telling anyone what to buy or sell.
  • Holding or moving anyone's money.
  • Audit opinions or tax advice.
  • Filing on anyone's behalf.
  • Keeping a company's books on our servers before hosting has had its security work.
