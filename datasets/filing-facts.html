Dataset

FilingFacts: an open dataset of SEC filing questions for AI

By Arhan Canli, Canli Capital. Free under CC BY 4.0.

2,243 questions on 402 companies with answers computed from SEC XBRL filings, for testing whether AI models get finance numbers right. Free under CC BY 4.0.

Results and tools

  • The FilingFacts benchmark scores AI models on the v0 questions, with and without a tool that reads the filings.
  • canli-mcp gives a model the filings themselves, so it can answer from the source.
  • The generator and scoring harness are open source.
  • The v0 dataset card has its method in full, and a citation.

Limits

  • Answers are computed from the companies' XBRL data and rechecked by a separate program. No expert has reviewed them yet; you can help check them.
  • XBRL can differ from the filing's text, and a company can tag a figure wrongly. A computed answer inherits that.
  • 15 restatement items in v1 ask for a year's figure but cite a shorter period. The errata lists them; score them out.
