Guards that cannot fire

By Arhan Canli, Canli Capital

Guards that cannot fire is one of 85 measurements this engine publishes in full. It is regenerated from a real run rather than transcribed, and it is shown here with its own claim boundary so the number and its limits arrive together.

What this measurement does and does not claim

A STRUCTURAL scan of source for checks that cannot fail. It reads code, not results: it cannot say whether a guard is correct, only whether it is capable of firing. Runs no backtest, opens no return data, registers no hypothesis. 0 trials.
