What this measurement does and does not claim

Retrospective diversification measurement of the exact current four-sleeve research specification and strategic overlay. It does not establish live-forward correlation, alpha, the 1.5 Sharpe objective, a reweighting decision, or retroactive admission. The return data were known before this protocol and the research window ends before the broker-reconciled forward record begins.
