The observation gate is bounded by the BOOK. correlation_observations is capped by the common window of candidate AND book, so minimum_oos_observations constrains admission through the BOOK's history as well as the candidate's. On the window measured here that cap is 728 days, below the 756 the contract requires. This is a real property of the gate, not a defect in the candidate: a book whose own common history is short cannot admit anything, however long the candidate's record. Recorded rather than lowered -- a correlation is the quantity this whole programme turns on, and measuring it on one year is the reason the minimum was raised in the first place.

Why two counts. The production combiner aligns to a contiguous epoch-day index and treats an absent day as flat, giving a window of 1061 days. A correlation can only be estimated from days on which both series are genuinely active, which is 728. correlation_observations means the second. Reporting only the first would overstate how well the correlation is known; reporting only the second understates how long the book has run.
