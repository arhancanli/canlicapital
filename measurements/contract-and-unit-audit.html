Contract and unit audit

By Arhan Canli, Canli Capital

What this measurement does and does not claim

Three audit dimensions worked against the contract in force and the published bundle. It reads configuration and artifacts; it runs no backtest, opens no return data, registers no hypothesis identity and changes no threshold. Findings are reported, not acted on. 0 trials.

Findings

Nested beyond what this page renders. Read it in the artifact.

Refutations are kept. A dimension that found nothing says so, with what it checked. An audit that reports only its hits cannot be told apart from one that did not run.
