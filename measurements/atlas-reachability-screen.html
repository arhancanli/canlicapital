Verdict meanings

HELD BUT BLOCKED ON EXTRACTION QUALITY. The documents are held and the shortfall is in reading them. C1's harness applies directly here: ask what a PERFECT detector would reach BEFORE writing a protocol.

HELD BUT REACHABILITY CEILING NOT MEASURED. The documents are held, but the frozen accuracy evidence needed to measure what a perfect detector could reach is incomplete. Parser work is not yet justified because the observed shortfall may be semantic rather than extractive.

HELD MACHINE GATES PASS HUMAN ACCURACY REQUIRED. The held corpus clears its frozen machine extraction gates, but the hash-bound human accuracy audit is incomplete. No classifier, return preregistration or sleeve claim is authorized until that independent evidence is complete.

HISTORY TOO SHORT FOR THE ADMISSION CONTRACT. The best source offers less history than the admission contract's minimum observations. The family cannot produce an admissible sleeve today no matter how good the idea is. It becomes screenable only by waiting, or by buying history.

NO POINT IN TIME RECORD WAS EVER PRESERVED. The as-of state was never archived; only the current or restated version survives. No amount of money or engineering recovers it. Forward collection from today is the only route, which means the clock starts now and the contract's minimum sets the wait.

OBTAINABLE FROM DATA THIS REPO ALREADY HOLDS. The record this identity needs is already in this repo's lake, with enough history for the contract. Nothing has to be bought and no new pipeline has to be built.

OBTAINABLE PUBLICLY NEEDS A NEW PIPELINE. The record exists and is public. The cost is engineering, which is the cheapest kind of blocker this programme has.

RECORD HELD BUT IDENTITY REDESIGN REQUIRED. The underlying record is held, but the measured gate does not describe one coherent population. Parser work cannot rescue it; a new identity and fresh preregistration are required before any further measurement.

RECORD OBTAINABLE BUT THE MARKS ARE NOT EXECUTABLE. The prices are assessed, indicated or broker-quoted rather than transacted. A backtest on them measures a price nobody could have traded, which is a more expensive kind of wrong than a null: it produces a number, and the number is not about trading.

VENDOR ONLY AN OWNER SPENDING DECISION. The record exists and is licensed. This is not an engineering question and it is not mine to answer; it is a spending decision with a number attached.

What is judged. Who holds a record this repo does NOT hold, and whether an as-of version of it was preserved at all. Every such row is stamped JUDGEMENT_NOT_VERIFIED_THIS_RUN and carries the concrete check that would settle it. None is presented as measured.

What is measured. The family list, derived from the atlas rather than listed. The contract minimum, read from the contract in force. The history of every source this repo holds, re-derived from the lake on this run. The verdict, computed from those.
