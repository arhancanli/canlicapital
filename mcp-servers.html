canli-mcp 0.22.0 · free and open source

Give your AI real financial data.

canli-mcp is a free finance MCP server that connects Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other AI assistants to SEC filings, market and economic data and 443 finance tools. Every answer says where its numbers came from. No key, no account.

Add it

One step, then ask

  1. In Claude or ChatGPT, as a connector

    Add a custom connector with this address. Nothing to install. The connector runs canli-mcp 0.21.0 and leaves out share prices and your own files; for those, and the newest release, run it on your computer.

    https://canlicapital.com/mcp/canli

  2. In Claude Code

    claude mcp add canli -- npx -y canli-mcp

  3. In every AI app on your computer at once

    It finds Claude Desktop, Cursor, VS Code and the others, adds one entry to each and keeps a copy of every file it changes.

    npx -y canli-mcp setup

Questions

Before you add it

Is it free?

Yes. canli-mcp is open source under the MIT licence. It needs no API key and no account, on your computer or as a connector.

Does it give investment advice?

No. It shows what companies filed and what the data says, with the source of each figure. What you do with it is your decision.

Where does the data come from?

From the people who publish it: the SEC's filings and their XBRL data, the Federal Reserve, the Treasury, the Bureau of Labor Statistics, FRED and others. Prices come from Yahoo Finance's public chart data unless you add your own Alpaca or Tiingo key.

What do I need to run it?

As a connector, nothing: paste the URL into Claude or ChatGPT. On your own computer, Node.js 20.10 or later. One command adds it to every AI app it finds.

Who makes it?

Arhan Canli, 15, in Dubai, in the open on GitHub. Corrections are published on the corrections page.
