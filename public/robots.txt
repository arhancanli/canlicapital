# Every page here is meant to be readable by people and by machines. Nothing is
# gated, nothing is cloaked, and no crawler is served different content.

# stay public and linked for people, the API and the MCP servers. General search
# crawlers skip them because every one is served noindex and the pages that cite
# them are what a search should find; skipping them keeps crawl time on those pages.
