Research

The current ALPHAC flagship combines 4 live sleeves: AlphaForge, AlphaMax, AlphaTrend, AlphaVintage. Their economic inputs differ, but diversification is not treated as proof of alpha. No sleeve clears the multiple-testing gate in-sample. AlphaVintage remains visible as a paper-trading history but its calendar-correct research verdict is KILLED; it is not an admitted sleeve. The live record, including a genuine risk-off episode, is the evidence that matters next.

Execution basis: paper trading and local simulation. “Live” below describes running systems, not funded execution. Model expectations are not measured forward returns. Inspect the forward evidence.

The data layer blocks leaks at the source, not after the fact.

The validation method in plain language: point-in-time data, purged walk-forward, deflation gates, pre-registration, and a byte-reproducible backtest.

04 / Crypto alone

Crypto alone is a null net of cost. Here is the proof.

Before the equity ballast, we ran crypto perpetuals through the deflation gates on their own. The result is NO-DEPLOY. The edge is too thin to survive cost.

crypto-perp strategies alone · 2021-01-01 to 2026-06-01 · 8 trials · best config A_blend

Capacity decay, annualised Sharpe by deployed capital

  • $100kSR 0.401
  • $1MSR 0.042
  • $10MSR -0.372

The edge decays as capital grows: 0.40 Sharpe at $100k, 0.04 at $1M, and -0.37 at $10M, where market impact consumes the thin signal. Crypto carry is finite-capacity alpha, not a scalable core.

NO-DEPLOY (honest null)

Best config DSR 0.2112 < 0.95 gate AND PBO 0.8818 > 0.20 gate. No variant beats baseline and clears deflation at once. Crypto-perps alone is a thin cross-sectional signal that does not survive honest deflation net of cost; the equity sleeve is required as ballast.

06 / Roadmap

The binding constraint is sample length, not idea count.

The binding constraint is trustworthy evidence, not idea count. The current four-sleeve core must earn its record forward while new candidates move through one-shot, pre-registered gates. The honest path is three-step.

  1. 01

    Earn the current book forward

    Accrue genuinely out-of-sample paper evidence on the four equal-quarter neutral sleeves, with the +10% strategic beta overlay reported separately. Do not relabel simulations as realized performance or rescue a weak result with an unregistered configuration change.

  2. 02

    Execute the registered discovery queue

    Test differentiated candidates across narrative, event, options, lending, power, credit, and ownership data under point-in-time, cost, capacity, deflation, and correlation gates. Publish ADD, KILL, or DATA-ESCALATE; never tune a failed one-shot identity after seeing returns.

  3. 03

    Buy data and infrastructure only after evidence

    Escalate to historical borrow/locate, options, broker, execution, or other paid credentials only when a named candidate has cleared every key-free screen. Credentials such as Alpaca belong to approved shadow execution, not to exploratory backtest rescue.
