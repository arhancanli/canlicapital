# Code of conduct

Canli Capital is a place to check claims, including our own. Newcomers are welcome, and so is
criticism of any published number, method or line of code.

## Expected

- Critique claims and methods, not people. Say what you checked and how.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that have been asked before.
- State uncertainty, and disclose conflicts of interest that bear on a review or a claim.
- Credit other people's work, and ask before naming someone who has not agreed to it.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including API keys, account data or contact details.
- Spam, solicitation, or promoting investment performance that published evidence does not support.

## Reporting

Send a private report through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/arhancanli/canlicapital/security/advisories/new) with
"Conduct" at the start of the title; only you and the repository's maintainers can see it. You can
also use GitHub's "Report content" option on any comment or issue, which goes to GitHub itself.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content, lock a discussion, or block a person from the
project when these standards are not met. The response fits the problem: a reminder for a first
small issue, a block for harassment.
